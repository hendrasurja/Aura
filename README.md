# Aura for iPhone

Mira, your AI companion, as a Home Screen app. Open **https://hendrasurja.github.io/aura/** in Safari,
tap **Share > Add to Home Screen**, then open Aura from your Home Screen.

- Hold the pink button to talk (Groq Whisper); she answers in her bubble and her voice (Groq Orpheus).
- Tap the keyboard to type instead. Tap Mira for a reaction. Tap "more" for the full answer.
- She searches the web with Gemini's Google Search and remembers things you tell her (Settings shows them).
- Your Gemini and Groq keys are stored only on your iPhone and sent only to Google and Groq.
  Nothing secret is in this repository.

Based on cloe-desktop (MIT licence). Mira's images are part of the Aura project.
